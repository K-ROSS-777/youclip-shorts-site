YouClip Shorts

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Summary

YouClip Shorts is a personal desktop application used only by its owner. It has no servers of its own. All data stays on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data the app accesses

When the owner connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the app may access:

  • Basic profile information (scope user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • Public video list (scope video.list): the account's public videos and their view counts, used to track the results of the owner's clips.
  • Video upload (scope video.upload): used to send videos the owner selects to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts. The app does not read private content.

The app accesses YouTube and Instagram data in a similar, limited way, only for accounts the owner has connected.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with the operating system's secure storage. Video statistics are stored in a local database on the same computer. Nothing is uploaded to any server other than the platform the owner is posting to.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. It is sent only to the platform it came from, for example when uploading a video to TikTok.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner disconnects the account or deletes the app's local data. Disconnecting removes the stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not used by anyone other than its adult owner.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
