YouClip Shorts

YouClip Shorts is a personal desktop application for Windows, used by a single creator to manage short-form video clipping work.

What it does

  • Finds and tracks clipping campaigns and their payout terms.
  • Keeps a record of the clips the creator makes for each campaign.
  • Uploads finished short videos to the creator's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. TikTok uploads arrive as drafts in the creator's TikTok inbox, where the creator edits and posts them.
  • Reads view counts of the creator's own public videos to track campaign results.

Who it is for

The app is not sold or offered to the public. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own social media accounts.
